About Zubr

Built for the intersection of rangeland science and practical conservation work.

We combine spectroscopy, remote sensing, and domain knowledge to help clients map, measure, and document what is happening on the land.

Talk to the team Explore our services

Our mission

Zubr Technologies builds data products and tools that make rangeland and conservation decisions faster, more objective, and better documented than traditional approaches.

We work across four service areas — mapping, forage analysis, rangeland conservation support, and technology and model development — because the problems our clients face rarely fit into a single category.

Team and collaborators

Cross-disciplinary expertise with field partners.

Founding team

Experience in spectroscopy, remote sensing, machine learning, and rangeland field operations.

Field collaborators

Ranchers, weed coordinators, land managers, and agency staff who shape what we build and how we deliver it.

Technical advisors

Experts in hyperspectral sensing, chemometrics, and rangeland ecology who support the technical roadmap.

Our vision

A platform that grows with the programs and problems it serves.

Data that supports decisions

From UAV maps to forage reports, outputs are designed around how clients actually use information — not how it looks in a demo.

Technology that earns trust

Calibrated, validated, and documented — so results can be used in program applications, not just internal notes.

Relationships over transactions

Most of our work comes from multi-year program engagements and referrals, not one-off sales.

What we stand for

Testable milestones

Clear targets and go/no-go criteria guide progress.

Field-first design

Deliverables are designed around real field constraints, not lab ideals or marketing specs.

Impact-driven

We focus on tools and data that help reduce invasive species pressure, protect intact rangeland, and make conservation programs more effective.

Want to work together?

We are looking for program partners, pilot engagements, and long-term collaborators.
