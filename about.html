About Zubr

Built for the intersection of rangeland science and practical conservation work.

We combine spectroscopy, remote sensing, and domain knowledge to help clients map, measure, and document what is happening on the land.

Talk to the team Explore our services

Our mission

Zubr Technologies turns rangeland imagery into grazing, forage, and treatment decisions. Founded in Montana in 2025 out of an effort to map the invasive annual grass ventenata, we build the field-validated bridge from hyperspectral remote sensing to the numbers land managers actually use.

Our durable asset is a proprietary, community-stratified calibration dataset, along with the workflow that carries forage-chemistry signals from bench measurements to UAV imagery to satellite reflectance. We sell finished reports and decision tools as a service to the advisors, districts, and cooperatives that manage western working lands.

Our vision

To become the standard, field-validated decision layer that turns rangeland imagery into grazing, forage, and treatment decisions.

Data that supports decisions

From UAV maps to forage reports, outputs are designed around how clients actually use information, not how it looks in a demo.

Technology that earns trust

Calibrated, validated, and documented, with per-pixel uncertainty, so results hold up in program applications, not just internal notes.

An asset that compounds

One engine, one growing calibration dataset across regions, seasons, and drought years, so every new customer strengthens the service for the rest.

What we stand for

Impact-driven
