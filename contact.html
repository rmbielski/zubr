A useful first note

Five details help us assess fit quickly.

  1. 01
    Decision

    What will the measurement change or inform?

  2. 02
    Place

    Region, approximate acreage, and number of pastures or treatment units.

  3. 03
    Target

    Biomass, forage chemistry, ventenata, change through time, or another variable.

  4. 04
    Evidence

    Existing field plots, laboratory results, UAV imagery, GIS boundaries, or management records.

  5. 05
    Timing

    Field season, decision deadline, and any reporting requirements.
