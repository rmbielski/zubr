ForageWatch

Pasture data, forage measurements, and herd demand in one workspace.

ForageWatch is Zubr’s rangeland intelligence product. It is being built to combine public satellite observations with private UAV and field data, then turn those inputs into pasture-level estimates that can be reviewed and exported.

The workspace above is the current product prototype—not a screenshot. Mapping, pasture management, data staging, and herd-demand calculations work now. Production prediction models are not connected yet.

Measurement workflow

A traceable path from field samples to management estimates.

The product is designed around model provenance, validation, and explicit uncertainty—not anonymous map layers.

01

Reference data

Field samples and laboratory chemistry establish the values used to train and test each target model.

02

Remote sensing

UAV hyperspectral and lidar data connect plot measurements to canopy-scale patterns; satellites provide broader repeat coverage.

03

Validation

Models are evaluated against held-out field observations before a layer is made available in the product.

04

Decision support

Validated layers can support pasture comparisons, grazing planning, monitoring, and documented field decisions.
