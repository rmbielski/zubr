Zubr Technologies

Turn rangeland imagery into grazing, forage, and treatment decisions.

Zubr is a rangeland-intelligence service. We fuse hyperspectral remote sensing, field-calibrated lab chemistry, and machine learning into forage-quality maps and decision reports for the advisors, districts, and cooperatives that manage western working lands.

What we deliver

Four service lines that share one engine and one growing calibration dataset — so every new customer strengthens the asset that serves the rest.

Forage Intelligence Report

Seasonal or one-time assessment of forage quantity, quality, and grazing capacity for a ranch, allotment, or treatment area.

Treatment ROI Monitoring

Before-and-after measurement of vegetation response and recovered forage value from weed control or restoration work.

Conservation Documentation

Program-ready baselines and before/after monitoring for NRCS-style conservation and Defend the Core work.

Advisor & Cooperative Platform

Multi-property monitoring with standardized, white-label reporting — and, over time, dashboard access.

How it works

A field-validated bridge from imagery to forage decisions.

Calibrate

Field crews collect grass samples; lab chemistry (silica, nitrogen, fiber) anchors our spectral calibration library.

Fly

Hyperspectral and lidar UAV surveys scale those signals from the bench to the canopy across your acres.

Model

Interpretable, uncertainty-aware models predict forage quality and biomass, and flag low-confidence pixels.

Decide

Maps become grazing-days, AUMs, nutritional-risk classes, and treatment-priority scores you can act on.
