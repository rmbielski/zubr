What we do

Four integrated services built on spectroscopy, remote sensing, and domain knowledge.

Ecosystem Mapping

Invasive species mapping, habitat characterization, and change detection via hyperspectral UAV and satellite data.

Forage & Rangeland Analysis

Crude protein, fiber content, biomass, and species composition from NIR and aerial data.

Rangeland Conservation Support

Baseline mapping and documentation for clients working with WLFW, NRCS, and related rangeland conservation programs.

Technology & Model Development

Custom sensor design, calibrations, and ML model R&D. The handheld NIR hay bale scanner is a working example.

How we work

Scoped to your goals, delivered with documentation.
