Services

Four integrated services for rangeland and conservation decision-making.

What we offer

Each service is scoped to your program, operation, or R&D goals. Most engagements combine two or more.

Ecosystem Mapping

Hyperspectral UAV surveys and satellite imagery for rangeland and habitat characterization.

  • Invasive species distribution and load mapping
  • Core rangeland delineation and habitat characterization
  • Change detection across seasons and years
  • GIS-compatible deliverables (shapefile, GeoTIFF, KMZ)
Request a mapping scope

Forage & Rangeland Analysis

Near-infrared spectroscopy and hyperspectral data for quantitative forage and range assessment.

  • Crude protein and ADF/NDF fiber content via NIR
  • Biomass estimation from aerial and ground data
  • Invasive species presence and load in hay and forage lots
  • Handheld NIR bale scanning — example: Ventenata detection
Discuss a forage project

Rangeland Conservation Support

Mapping and documentation for clients working within WLFW Defend the Core, NRCS, and related conservation programs.

  • Core intact rangeland mapping and encroachment monitoring
  • Vulnerability assessments for program planning
  • Baseline data and reporting to support program applications
  • Program-formatted deliverables for agency review
Talk about your program

Technology & Model Development

Custom sensor engineering and ML model R&D for spectroscopy and remote sensing applications.

  • Custom NIR and hyperspectral sensor design
  • Dataset collection and calibration for client-specified targets
  • PLS, PLS-DA, SVM, and neural network model development
  • Example: handheld hay bale scanner for Ventenata detection
