Services

Four service lines, one rangeland-intelligence engine.

Zubr sells finished rangeland reports and decision tools as a service to the advisors, districts, cooperatives, and agencies that manage many properties at once. Each line is powered by the same calibrated dataset and models.

What we offer

Each line is scoped to your program or operation. Most customers start with one and grow into the platform.

Forage Intelligence Report

A seasonal or one-time assessment of forage quantity, quality, and grazing capacity for a ranch, allotment, or treatment area.

  • Crude protein, fiber (NDF), and silica by pasture
  • Biomass and available grazing-days / AUMs
  • Invasive annual grass presence and load
  • Nutritional-risk classes and intake-limitation flags
Request a forage report

Treatment ROI Monitoring

Measures vegetation response and recovered forage value before and after weed control or restoration work.

  • Before/after change detection on treated units
  • Recovered forage value and forage-days regained
  • Treatment-priority scores across your acres
  • Break-even and cost-per-additional-AUM estimates
Measure a treatment

Conservation Documentation

Program-ready baselines and before/after monitoring for NRCS-style conservation and Defend the Core work.

  • Core intact rangeland mapping and encroachment monitoring
  • Vulnerability assessments for program planning
  • Baseline data and reporting for program applications
  • Program-formatted, GIS-compatible deliverables
Talk about your program

Advisor & Cooperative Platform

Multi-property monitoring for the intermediaries who serve dozens or hundreds of landowners at once.

  • Standardized, white-label reporting across properties
  • One contract that reaches many acres and allotments
  • Recurring seasonal monitoring and trend tracking
  • Dashboard and interface access over time
